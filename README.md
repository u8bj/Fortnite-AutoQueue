# Included German recognition templates

Try the supplied crops and config.json first. No new screenshots are required
unless recognition fails with your game language, UI layout or resolution.
Use **Test recognition** before relying on the preset. Capture replacements
only for cues that do not work on your screen.

These are small crops of the German Fortnite interface. Fortnite assets are
owned by their respective rights holders and are not covered by the source
code's MIT license. Full personal game screenshots are not included.
